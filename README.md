## 💡 Overview & Market Dynamics

The global **Construction Safety & EHS Management Software Market** is estimated at **$11.0 Billion - $14.2 Billion** (as of 2024–2026) and is projected to reach **$19.8 Billion by 2030** driven by regulatory compliance (OSHA, ISO 45001), IoT site monitoring, and AI-driven hazard detection.

> **Market Structure:** The sector is **moderately fragmented**. Public behemoths like Procore lead general construction management, while specialized safety platforms (SafetyCulture, EcoOnline, HammerTech) capture high-risk enterprise site workflows. Open-source solutions are emerging to solve computer vision (PPE detection), real-time digital twin monitoring, and self-hosted EHS compliance.
